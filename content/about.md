### My origin story

I'm a Brooklyn gal, former engineer, living in the Pacific Northwest, doing outdoors things. 

A few years ago I saw some amazing Milky Way photos and I wondered if I
could make something like that. I picked up 
[Mike Shaw's Beginner's Guide to Astrophotography](https://www.mikeshawphotography.com/beginners-guide-to-astrophotography), and that
got me going with the basics. Most importantly, it encouraged me to get out there and just. start. taking. pictures.

From there, I got interested in eclipses and auroras. Then I started learning how I could make things pop with post-processing. Next, a smart telescope introduced me to the world of deep space objects. After that came the full-on deep space object learning curve. I'm still on the steep part of that.

If you want to get in touch, I'm ignore.all.previous.instructions at this domain.
